Terms & Conditions

RA 10173

CHARRMPASS • Aklan State University – Ibajay Campus

Official Campus Access & Registration Agreement

Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before submitting your registration to CHARRMPASS (Web-Based Smart Entry and Exit Monitoring System Using RFID Technology for Aklan State University – Ibajay Campus).

1 System Purpose & Scope

CHARRMPASS regulates and monitors pedestrian and vehicular entry and exit at Aklan State University – Ibajay Campus using Dual-Frequency RFID technology (13.56 MHz Close-Range Cards for pedestrians and UHF Long-Range Stickers for vehicles). Registration is required for students, faculty, staff, and authorized stakeholders to obtain a CPASS ID and RFID credentials.

2 Data Privacy & Protection (Republic Act No. 10173)

In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, Aklan State University – Ibajay Campus acts as the Personal Information Controller for data submitted through CHARRMPASS. By registering, you freely and expressly consent to the collection, storage, and processing of the following information:

  • Personal & Identification Data: Full name, age, sex, residential address, academic or employment role, program/department, section, Student ID / CPASS ID, profile photo, and identification cards (front and back).
  • Vehicle & Access Data: Vehicle type, model, plate number, color, optional OR/CR, Driver's License, motorcycle photo, RFID tag assignments, and automated gate entry/exit timestamps.
  • Purpose & Security Safeguards: Your data is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS, stored in access-controlled cloud storage and databases, and used strictly by authorized ASU – Ibajay Campus administrators and security personnel for identity verification, gate access control, and campus safety auditing. Personal data is never sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.
  • Data Retention & Disposal: Registration records and gate access logs are retained only for the duration of your active enrollment, employment, or authorized campus access and in accordance with university recordkeeping policies, after which they are securely archived or permanently deleted.
  • Your Rights as a Data Subject (Sec. 16, RA 10173): You have the right to be informed, to access your personal records, to request correction (rectification) of inaccurate details, to object or withdraw consent (which will deactivate campus RFID access), to request erasure or blocking of your data, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
  • Privacy Inquiries & Data Requests: To exercise your data privacy rights or request updates/deletion of your records, please contact the ASU – Ibajay Campus Security Office, CHARRMPASS System Administrator, or the Campus Data Protection Officer (DPO).

3 Accuracy & Authenticity of Submitted Documents

You affirm that all personal details, vehicle records, and uploaded photographs/documents are true, current, and authentic. Submitting falsified, altered, or another person's identification or vehicle documents is a serious offense that will result in immediate rejection or revocation of campus access privileges and may be referred to university disciplinary authorities.

4 CPASS ID, RFID Card & UHF Sticker Usage

  • Your CPASS ID, close-range RFID card, and long-range UHF vehicle sticker are strictly non-transferable and may only be used by the registered person or affixed to the specific registered vehicle.
  • Lending, swapping, or tampering with RFID tags to grant unauthorized entry or bypass anti-passback monitoring is strictly prohibited.
  • Lost, stolen, or damaged RFID cards or UHF stickers must be reported immediately to the ASU – Ibajay Campus Security Office or System Administrator for deactivation.

5 Campus Gate, Traffic & Parking Regulations

  • All registrants must tap in upon entry and tap out upon exit at the designated campus gates so campus occupancy records remain accurate.
  • Vehicle owners must slow down at the Entry and Exit UHF zones, cooperate with on-duty security guards for visual photo/plate verification, and park only in designated campus parking areas.
  • The university is not liable for loss or damage to vehicles or personal belongings left unattended inside parked vehicles on campus premises.

6 Administrative Review & Right to Revoke Access

Submitting this registration places your account in PENDING status until verified and approved by a Campus Administrator. ASU – Ibajay Campus Administration and Security reserve the right to deny applications, perform manual gate overrides, or suspend/revoke RFID authorization at any time for non-compliance with university security policies.
